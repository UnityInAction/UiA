Earth Day (Kindergarten)

In the sequence of Earth Day celebrations, this year Unity in Action was taking opportunities beyond the Senior High School. As a club that shares environmental awareness and takes a commitment to take care of the environment, we were proud to share our club’s dedication for wider segments. Besides the Earth day’s celebration such as booth games and workshops, we comply to share our dedication to Kindergarteners and Nurseries as Santa Laurensia School. As part of the club, we taught them how to plant and take care of hydroponics, directly at the green house.

During the April month, Unity in Action was welcoming kindergarteners and nurseries in our Senior High corridor, in front of the first Biology Lab. Their arrival was divided into three separate days which was on the 13th, 14th and 16th April, during the school day. Each day consisted of around three to four classes accompanied by their homeroom teachers and us as the UIA’s members. Each class was divided into two stations. The first station was about media planting. In this station, the students were taught to place cloth into a net pot which became the medium of planting. Beforehand, UIA’s members tried to hide the missing pots surrounding the place so that the students could try to find the missing pieces. Not only that, we also tried to demonstrate the step-by-step procedure in placing the right cloth in the pot, and placing rock wool as the seed medium. Through this process, we as the UIA’s members were glad to be able to see the enthusiasm and interest of the students.

After the media plantation teachings, the students were asked to visit the greenhouse. Inside the place, we showed the students the different types of plants such as mustard plants, (K). Not only that, we also showed parts of the hydroponics such as the roots, stem and leaves.

In conclusion, spreading environmental awareness in the form of hydroponics became a good tool to teach our little fellows regarding the importance of saving the environment.
